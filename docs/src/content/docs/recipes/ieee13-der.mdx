The IEEE 13-node test feeder, extended with distributed generation. The
substation node 650 feeds the trunk through a voltage regulator
(`transformer` with `oltc: true`), XFM-1 steps node 634 down to 480 V,
and the 671–692 tie is a closed `switch`. Four DER units connect
through their own step-up transformers, all drawn with
[`plant`](/symbols/generation/plant/): a wind park (`wind3`) at 646,
PV + battery (`pv-bess`) behind XFM-1 at 634, a PV park (`pv3`) at 680
and a battery (`bess2`) at 675.

Buses along the horizontal runs are drawn as _vertical_ bars so each
run taps their interiors perpendicularly — no conductor ever lands on a
bus end. The junction buses (632, 671) stay horizontal and the laterals
reach them with a single `elbow` onto an interior tap (`bus-frac`);
loads leave the vertical bars with elbowed leads.
