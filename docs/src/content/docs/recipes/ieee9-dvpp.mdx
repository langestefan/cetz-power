A modified **IEEE nine-bus** transmission system with an MV distribution grid
hung underneath. Three synchronous machines (**SG1–SG3**) sit behind buses 1–3;
the classic 4–9 network carries the firm loads. The **DVPP area** (a dynamic
virtual power plant — buses **d1a/d1b** and **d2–d8**) connects to the
transmission grid at **buses 4 and 6** through the two **POC** transformers, and
inside it a **wind** plant, a battery (**BESS**) and a **PV** plant each tie in
through their own transformer. The whole figure was digitised from the source,
so the relative geometry matches the original.

## How it's built

- **Digitised from a pixel map.** A single helper `P(x, y) = (x * s, (H - y) * s)`
  maps source-image pixels to canvas coordinates — flipping the image's y-down
  axis to CeTZ's y-up — so every [`bus`](/symbols/grid/bus/),
  [`transformer`](/symbols/grid/transformer/) and conductor reads straight off
  the measured pixel numbers (`bus("b7", P(281, 38), P(281, 83))`). The scale
  `s` is the master **compression** knob: shrinking it shortens every line while
  the fixed-size symbols stay put.
- **Synchronous machines reuse a stock symbol.** SG1–SG3 are
  [`voltagesource`](/symbols/electrical/voltagesource/) circles with
  `kind: "sin"` (the circle-with-sine generator), wrapped in a small `sg(...)`
  closure that adds the grey fill and a caption placed *outside* the circle.
- **Transformers double as conductors.** The transmission-line banks and the two
  vertical **POC** transformers are stock two-node
  [`transformer`](/symbols/grid/transformer/)s — their auto-drawn leads *are* the
  line into each bus, landing on bus centres. The three small device
  transformers feed the wind/BESS/PV blocks.
- **Every join is perpendicular.** The 4→5 and 4→6 funnels never meet a bar at an
  angle: each leaves its bus with a short vertical stub, runs the diagonal in the
  middle, then drops a matching stub into bus 4 — and the two legs are exact
  **mirror images** about bus 4's centre (where the load arrow sits).
- **Taps are placed deliberately.** Connections tap bus *interiors* (often the
  centre); where two conductors leave one bar on opposite sides they share a
  single collinear vertical; and a bar's taps are spread evenly (d8 carries three
  equidistant taps — transformer→left, the d7 link→centre, the load→right).
- **The DVPP boundary.** A dashed background `rect` groups the distribution grid;
  it is drawn first so it sits behind everything. Leads cross its edge, but the
  POC transformer symbols are kept clear of it. The coloured **wind / BESS / PV**
  blocks are plain `rect`s, each wired to its transformer so nothing floats.
- **Loads** are small [`load`](/symbols/loads/load/) arrows aimed with `angle:`
  (and `elbow:` for the up-then-right MV loads), anchored at the bus they tap.
