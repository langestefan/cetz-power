A medium-voltage transport connection: a feeding substation
(*Onderstation*) steps a high-voltage source down through a TS/MS
transformer onto its OS-MS busbar, then carries the power to a switching
station (*Schakelstation*) over a six-cable MV link. Inside the
Schakelstation a coupling field (*Koppelveld*) ties the incoming RS-MS bar
to the outgoing RS-MS bar, which fans out to the departing feeders. Each
node carries its load-flow result.

## How it's built

- The diagram is a grid of **vertical busbars** (`bus` placed with two
  endpoints, so the length is implicit) joined entirely by
  [`multi-wire`](/helpers/feeder/): the OS-MS → RS-MS link is one
  bus-to-bus call, the **Koppelveld** is a single bus coupler
  (`count: 1`), and the **departing feeders** are a `multi-wire` *stub fan*
  — passing an offset `(out-len, 0)` instead of a target bus draws the
  cables into open space, since there's no facing bar on the right.
  Because the bars overshoot their cables (see below), every call lands on
  the same fractional `band`, so all the cables line up.
- The **HV feed** is drawn green: a [`machine`](/symbols/generation/machine/)
  source, a short [`bus`](/symbols/grid/bus/), and the
  [`wire`](/symbols/grid/wire/)s take `stroke: g`; the
  [`transformer`](/symbols/grid/transformer/) keeps its **MS-side** winding
  black via `secondary-stroke: black`. The whole feed sits half a cable-gap
  low, so its lead meets the OS-MS bar *between* the top two cables — a fork.
- The four busbars share one **top edge**: each tall bar overshoots its
  cables by `over = ts-h/2 - gap/2` at the top and the same at the bottom, so
  its top lands exactly at the short TS bar's top (see the
  [`bus`](/symbols/grid/bus/) reference).
- The two **station envelopes** are plain dashed `cetz.draw.rect`s drawn
  first (so the cables sit on top), each with a large title. The MV cables
  deliberately cross the box edges — the link leaves the Onderstation and
  enters the Schakelstation.
- Every callout is a [`note`](/helpers/feeder/) or a `cetz.draw.content`
  anchored beside the bar it annotates; the layout is fully parametric
  (`x-*` columns, `gap`, `n` cables), so changing the spacing or adding a
  cable is a one-line change.

:::tip[`multi-wire` stub fans]
`multi-wire`'s `target` can be a bus name **or** a `(dx, dy)` offset. With an
offset it draws free stubs off the source bar — exactly the departing-feeder
case, where the cables leave RS-MS uitgaand with no facing bus to land on.
That's what lets the whole figure use one helper for every cable group.
:::
