The variable-speed counterpart to the [fixed-speed wind
turbine](/recipes/wind-turbine/). A **doubly-fed induction generator
(DFIG)** feeds the grid from its stator _and_ exchanges slip power
through its rotor via a back-to-back converter. A three-winding
transformer does the splitting: the HV winding ties to the grid, one
secondary feeds the stator path, and the other feeds the rotor path.

<Snippet name="recipe-wind-turbine-dfig" />

## How it's built

A few composition patterns carry most of the diagram:

- **The split** is a single [`transformer3`](/symbols/grid/transformer3/)
  placed on the main axis. Its `hv` terminal takes the feed from the
  _Trafo MS_ bus; `lv` and `tv` fan up and down into the two branches
  (routed with `elbow(..., corner: "v")` for a clean riser-then-run).
- **The cable boxes** (_Mastvoet_ → _Gondel_) are not a special symbol —
  each is two short buses closed top and bottom by `multi-wire(...,
count: 2)`, which lands one strand at each bus end and so draws the
  rectangle. A small local `cable-box` closure stamps both.
- **The P/Q callouts** under the left buses reuse the same `flow-bus`
  closure as the fixed-speed recipe — a bus plus two corner `note`s.
- **The converter** and the **turbine envelope** are plain
  `cetz.draw.rect` calls with a dashed stroke; the converter's wedge is
  just two `cetz.draw.line`s.
