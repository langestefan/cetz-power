A whole-network overview of reactive-power flow direction. An HV network
(modelled as an [external grid](/symbols/grid/external-grid/)) plus a
synchronous generator feed the **T-D interface**; three HV/MV transformers
step down to the MV conductor, three MV/LV transformers step down to the LV
feeders, and **DALI** sensors meter the LV interface. The middle path carries
direction arrows — Q₁ (solid →) flows TSO→DSO, Q₂ (dashed ⇠) DSO→TSO.

<Snippet name="recipe-reactive-flow" />

## How it's built

- The **HV network** is an [`external-grid`](/symbols/grid/external-grid/)
  rotated to point right (`angle: 90deg`) with a grey `background`; the
  **SG** is a [`voltagesource`](/symbols/electrical/voltagesource/) with
  `kind: "ac"`, which draws the sine-in-a-circle generator symbol, tapped
  below the feed with its own short `wire`.
- The **T-D interface** and the **MV node** are plain vertical
  [`wire`](/symbols/grid/wire/)s, *not* busbars — thin conductors that join
  the three transformers at each voltage level. The "T-D interface" callout
  points at the midpoint of the left conductor, where the HV feed enters
  between the transformer banks.
- Each bank is three [`transformer`](/symbols/grid/transformer/)s placed
  two-node between a conductor and the next column. A small `qpair(cx, y)`
  closure draws the solid-→ / dashed-⇠ flow arrows on the middle row with
  `cetz.draw.line(..., mark: (end: ">"))`.
- The LV feeder is a `wire` carrying three [`load`](/symbols/loads/load/)
  arrows; the **DALI** sensor is a labelled `rect` tapped off the line
  through two small metering circles. The **TSO/DSO** area shading is two
  background `rect`s split at the T-D interface, and the legend reuses the
  same arrow styles as the flow markers.
