A hybrid renewable park at its point of connection: the external grid
feeds the 10 kV collector bus through an OLTC `transformer` with a
metering `ct`, and three plant bays connect the wind park (`wind3`),
the PV park (`pv3`) and a converter-coupled battery (`bess2`), each
drawn with [`plant`](/symbols/generation/plant/). The bus also carries
a fused capacitor bank, the park auxiliaries and a normally-open ring
tie (`junction(open: true)`), and a park controller closes a dashed
signal loop — CT measurement in, battery dispatch out.

The layout choices that keep it compact: the plant bays sit on
_equidistant interior taps_ and share one reusable `bay()` closure
(only the middle element — transformer or converter — differs), the
figure stays mirror-symmetric around the grid infeed, the bus is drawn
_last_ so the bar covers the lead roots, and the signal path hugs the
right edge so it crosses no conductors.
