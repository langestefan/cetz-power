The realistic **110 kV distribution grid** used to study reactive-power
redispatch at the TSO-DSO interface: 34 stations (**S_1–S_34**) meshed
between two **PCC substations** where the grid couples to the transmission
system — **M-Stadt** (a double-busbar station) and **K-Dorf** (a single bar
whose two tie circuits to S_22 are drawn in amber). **S_24** and **S_14**
are the other double-busbar hubs; generation plants are the circle-with-sine
sources, and connections to neighbouring subsystems leave the figure as
arrows.

<Snippet name="recipe-tso-dso-110kv" />

## How it's built

- **Digitised from a pixel map.** One helper `P(x, y) = (x * s, (H - y) * s)`
  maps source-figure pixels to canvas coordinates, so every
  [`bus`](/symbols/grid/bus/) and conductor reads straight off the measured
  pixel numbers, and the scale `s` compresses the whole layout in one place.
  The routing itself is a cleaned-up redraw: every run is orthogonal and every
  join lands perpendicularly on a bar interior.
- **Double busbars are two `bus` bars plus junction pairs.** A bay crosses
  both bars as one wire; a `jpair(x, ya, yb, sel)` helper then drops a
  **filled** [`junction`](/symbols/grid/junction/) on the bar the bay selects
  and an **open** one (the hollow netopening dot) on the other — the
  DIgSILENT-style busbar-selection look. Bus couplers are stepped links — the
  left leg on the top bar, the right leg on the bottom bar — with a
  [`breaker`](/symbols/protection/breaker/) in the bridge.
- **Twin drops onto paired lines.** Where a station ties into a pair of
  parallel runs (S_1 onto the S_2/S_9 lines, S_26 onto the two S_25 ties,
  S_14 down to the two S_18 lines), two collinear verticals leave it: the
  left one terminates on the top line, the right one runs through to the
  bottom line — the crossing stays junction-free.
- **Junctions come last.** Wires are drawn first, then the bars (so the thick
  bars cover the bay roots), then the junction dots — an open junction's white
  fill masks whatever runs beneath it, which is exactly what a netopening
  should do (the dashed S*29 link is the one \_open* line section).
- **Plants reuse stock symbols.** Every generation plant is a
  [`voltagesource`](/symbols/electrical/voltagesource/) circle with
  `kind: "sin"`; the one synchronous generator at S_22 is a
  [`machine`](/symbols/generation/machine/) with a **G** body. Station loads
  are hollow [`load`](/symbols/loads/load/) arrows (`fill: white`).
- **Subsystem departures are annotations.** The arrows leaving the figure at
  S*10, S_19, S_22–S_26 and below S_24 are
  [`flow-arrow`](/helpers/flow-arrow/)s with an italic \_Subsystem*
  `note` caption where the original labels one — they are not
  conductors.
- **The PCC areas are borderless backdrops.** Two peach
  [`area`](/helpers/area/)s (`stroke: none`) sit behind the M-Stadt and
  K-Dorf bars, drawn first; the bold **PCC** captions hang outside them. The
  K-Dorf↔S_22 double circuit keeps the source figure's amber colour via plain
  `cetz.draw.line` strokes.
