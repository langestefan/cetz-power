The control scheme of a grid-side converter, reproducing Figure 8 of
[this IEEE paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10054041):
solid lines are power connections, dashed lines are signal
connections. Power flows from the
sources on the DC bus through the DC link (a boxed `capacitor`, the
V<sub>DC</sub> measurement point), the `converter` (`kind: "dc-ac"`),
an LC filter and a grid inductor to the PCC bar. The outer-loop
reference generator tracks the P/Q setpoints and the DC-link voltage;
the inner PI loop drives the converter; a PLL synchronises the
abc → dq0 measurement frame at the filter capacitor.

Techniques worth stealing: every dashed signal is a `flow-arrow` (its
arrowhead is rebuilt solid, dodging the dashed-mark gotcha) at the end
of a plain dashed polyline; the filter passives are the one-node
`inductor` / `capacitor` symbols laid sideways with `angle:` inside
plain `block` boxes; the multiplication point is a `mixer` (the
control-notation circled ×); and the inactive power sources are just
grey-stroked blocks with grey body text.
