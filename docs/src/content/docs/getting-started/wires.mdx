Use `wire(a, b)` to connect two points with a straight line, or
`elbow(a, b, corner: "h")` for an L-shaped routing that goes
horizontally first, then vertically (use `corner: "v"` for the
opposite).

<Snippet name="getting-started-wires-elbows" />

Wires aren't full "symbols" — they have no style family of their own;
their stroke follows the global `cetz-power.wire.stroke`. They do take
an inline `label:`, which lands on the wire's free side by default
(north above a horizontal wire, turned upright beside a vertical one) —
see the [Wires reference](/symbols/grid/wire/) for the label arguments
and the `elbow(stub:)` join.

## Restyling every wire at once

Set `cetz-power.wire.stroke` once and every `wire` and `elbow` in the
canvas picks it up. Symbol bodies keep their own stroke — only the
wires change.
